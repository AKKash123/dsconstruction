Customized architecture design

Every brief becomes a tailored design — your site, your light, your way of living, translated into drawings and 3D walkthroughs you can sign off before we pour a single footing.

01 · Discovery

Site study and brief

We walk the site with you, study orientation, soil, light, views and local regulations, then capture how you actually want to live or work in the space. The output is a written brief and a measured site plan.

02 · Concept

Massing and concept design

Two or three concept directions — floor plans, sections and rough massing — are presented in a single workshop. You pick a direction (or a hybrid) and we lock the concept before drawing anything detailed.

03 · 3D visualization

Walkthrough and material palette

The chosen concept is built in 3D with realistic light and materials. You walk through every room, lift ceilings, move walls and approve the finish palette — timber, stone, paint chips and hardware — long before construction starts.

04 · Documentation

Working drawings and BOQ

Structural, MEP, joinery and finishing drawings are issued for tender, with a bill of quantities and a fixed-price build quote from our construction team. One set of documents, one accountable team.

05 · Handover to build

Seamless transition to construction

The same team that designed your project builds it. The architect stays on through execution, answering RFIs and approving shop drawings, so what you approved in 3D is what you get keys to.
